Parametric Surrogate Modeling
Virtual metrology for a post-implant RTA step — worked example

Overview

This example walks through how to replace an expensive process+device simulator with a fast, physics-shaped surrogate that a fab can call inline — for virtual metrology, recipe optimization, and yield recovery. The process is intentionally narrow so the workflow stays crisp: a single post-implant Rapid Thermal Anneal (RTA) step for a shallow boron source/drain extension, with two electrical KPIs (sheet resistance $R_s$ and junction depth $X_j$).

Model scope and known limitations

Half the value of a surrogate is knowing where it lies. This worked example is calibrated to a synthetic TCAD stand-in; a production deployment would require re-calibration to real metrology data and additional validation gates before influencing process decisions.

  • Gas chemistry. The pressure knob is O$_2$ partial pressure (balance N$_2$). Other ambient species (H$_2$O, H$_2$, NH$_3$) are implicitly fixed; a multi-chamber deployment would need per-chemistry calibration or additional partial-pressure inputs.
  • Thermal-budget proxy. The constraint uses $D_{\text{eff}}(T,t,\text{dose})\cdot t$ evaluated at the end of the ramp. This is a good approximation in the spike ($t \ll \tau_{311}$) and soak ($t \gg \tau_{311}$) limits but underestimates budget slightly at intermediate dwell times when TED is still active.
  • Surrogate accuracy at the optimum. The constrained optimizer can steer toward knob-bound corners where training density is lower. Re-simulating the recommended recipe is always advisable before committing to a process change.

The four-step workflow

  1. Sparse, smart sampling. Latin Hypercube the recipe box so every projection of the input space is uniformly covered. Each expensive simulation buys maximum information.
  2. Fit equations you already trust. The functional form comes from device physics — Arrhenius diffusion, stretched activation kinetics, mobility roll-off. Only the coefficients are learned from data.
  3. Evaluate everywhere. The fitted model is a few lines of arithmetic, so any new recipe is answered in microseconds. Now you can sweep, score, and search.
  4. Optimize under real constraints. Wrap the surrogate in an SLSQP solver with the actual process limits (thermal budget, junction-depth ceiling, knob bounds) and pull yield back into spec.

Step 1 — Sparse sampling of the expensive simulator

The "expensive" simulator stands in for a coupled process+device TCAD run that takes minutes to hours per point. We can only afford a few dozen evaluations, so each one has to buy information rather than provide locally-redundant coverage.

Latin Hypercube Sampling

LHS divides each input axis into $N$ equal-probability strata and permutes points so that each stratum is hit exactly once. The result: every 1-D projection is uniformly covered with $N$ points, and 2-D projections are far better-spread than i.i.d. random.

For knobs that span orders of magnitude (dose, time, pressure) we sample in log-space so the underlying multiplicative physics is well-conditioned.

Why not full factorial?

4 knobs × 5 levels = 625 runs. At 30 minutes per CAE point that's almost two weeks of compute for a single sweep. LHS at $N{=}40$ gives a calibrated surrogate good to ~1% on the held-out set (see Step 3) and finishes overnight.

The 40-point LHS plan (projections)

Code

from scipy.stats import qmc

def latin_hypercube(n, bounds, log_knobs, seed=7):
    sampler = qmc.LatinHypercube(d=len(bounds), seed=seed)
    u = sampler.random(n=n)                       # uniform [0,1]^d
    out = {}
    for j, (name, (lo, hi)) in enumerate(bounds.items()):
        if name in log_knobs:
            out[name] = np.exp(np.log(lo) + u[:, j]*(np.log(hi) - np.log(lo)))
        else:
            out[name] = lo + u[:, j]*(hi - lo)
    return pd.DataFrame(out)

Step 2 — Fit physics-shaped equations, not a black box

For RTA of an implanted dopant, the functional form is dictated by decades of device physics. We fit coefficients; we do not invent topology.

Surrogate equations

Equilibrium diffusivity (Arrhenius):

$$D_{\text{eq}}(T) \;=\; D_0\,\exp\!\left(-\dfrac{E_{a,\text{diff}}}{k_B T}\right)$$

Transient enhanced diffusion (TED) — {311} cluster dissolution:

$$\tau_{311}(T) \;=\; \tau_0^{311}\,e^{\,E_a^{311}/k_BT}, \qquad E_{\text{TED}}(T,t,\text{dose}) \;=\; 1 + A_{\text{TED}}\,\left(\dfrac{\text{dose}}{\text{dose}_0}\right)^{\!p_{\text{TED}}}\,e^{-t/\tau_{311}(T)}$$

$$D_{\text{eff}}(T,t,\text{dose}) \;=\; D_{\text{eq}}(T)\cdot E_{\text{TED}}(T,t,\text{dose})$$

Activation fraction (stretched-exponential kinetics):

$$f_{\text{act}}(T,t) \;=\; 1 - \exp\!\left[-\left(\dfrac{t}{\tau_0\,e^{\,E_{a,\text{act}}/k_BT}}\right)^{\beta}\right]$$

Dose-dependent junction depth (Gaussian tail crossing substrate background $N_{bg}$):

$$C_s = \dfrac{\text{dose}\cdot f_{\text{act}}}{\sqrt{\pi\,D_{\text{eff}}\,t}}, \qquad X_j = \sqrt{4\,D_{\text{eff}}\,t\,\ln\!\left(\dfrac{C_s}{N_{bg}}\right)}$$

Mobility roll-off (Caughey–Thomas form, with $N_a = \text{dose}\cdot f_{\text{act}}/X_j$):

$$\mu(N_a) \;=\; \dfrac{\mu_0}{1 + (N_a / N_{\text{ref}})^{\gamma}}$$

Sheet resistance with weak surface-oxidation perturbation (O$_2$ partial pressure):

$$R_s \;=\; \dfrac{1}{q\,\text{dose}\,f_{\text{act}}\,\mu}\,\left(\dfrac{p_{O_2}}{p_{O_2}^{\text{ref}}}\right)^{p_{\text{exp}}}$$

Twelve unknown coefficients $\theta=\{\log D_0, E_{a,\text{diff}},\,\log A_{\text{TED}}, p_{\text{TED}}, \log\tau_0^{311}, E_a^{311},\,\log\tau_0, E_{a,\text{act}}, \beta,\,\log\mu_0, \gamma,\,p_{\text{exp}}\}$ are fit by bounded nonlinear least squares on $\log R_s$ and $\log X_j$.

Fitted coefficients

Three modeling philosophies, side by side

Physics-only

Eight interpretable coefficients with units. Extrapolates gracefully because the Arrhenius shape is the truth at first order. Bias-limited where second-order effects (tool seasoning, chamber drift, edge wafer) live.

Neural-net (black box)

Small MLP on log-transformed inputs. Fits anything, but memorizes the LHS cloud, has zero opinion about Arrhenius shape, and degrades fast outside the training box.

Hybrid (physics + residual NN)

Physics carries the bulk trend and extrapolation; a tiny NN learns the multiplicative residual where reality and model disagree. This is the pattern most production virtual-metrology stacks land on.

def residuals(theta, df, Rs_meas, Xj_meas):

Step 3 — Evaluate everywhere, score honestly

Step 4 — Optimize the recipe under real constraints

A surrogate that's cheap to call lets us put the constraints inside the optimizer rather than catching violations after the fact.

Optimization problem

$$\min_{\text{dose},\,T,\,t,\,p_{O_2}}\;\bigl(R_s(\text{dose},T,t,p_{O_2})-R_s^{\text{target}}\bigr)^{2}$$

subject to

$$X_j(\text{dose},T,t,p_{O_2}) \le X_j^{\max},\quad D_{\text{eff}}(T,t,\text{dose})\cdot t \le (Dt)^{\max},\quad \text{knobs in their bounds.}$$

The $Dt$-budget constraint (cm$^2$) is the dimensionally-correct replacement for the old $T\cdot t$ form — it correctly penalizes spike anneals that would otherwise slip past a linear $T\cdot t$ cap. Solved with SLSQP from many random restarts to dodge local minima.

Recommended recipe (hybrid surrogate)

Code

cons = [
    {"type": "ineq", "fun": lambda x: XJ_MAX - predict_fn(_df(x))["Xj"][0]},

]

def objective(x):
    return (predict_fn(_df(x))["Rs"][0] - RS_TARGET)**2

Where AI models and agents fit in the live fab

During fitting (off-line)

  • Form proposal. An LLM-backed agent reads recent process notes, defect-engineering literature, and the existing compact-model library, and proposes candidate functional forms (e.g. add a $\sqrt{t}$ transient-enhanced-diffusion term) for the modeling engineer to accept or reject.
  • Bayesian active learning. A Gaussian-process acquisition agent looks at the current surrogate's uncertainty map and recommends the next 2–5 expensive simulations or split-lot wafers to run. This typically cuts the wafer cost of a calibrated model by 3–5x relative to a fixed DOE.
  • Symbolic regression. Tools like PySR or AI-Feynman search a constrained algebra of physically-meaningful operators for terms the human compact model is missing — useful for catching second-order effects before they become yield excursions.
  • Coefficient sanity agent. A guardrail LLM checks that fitted activation energies, prefactors, and exponents fall within tabulated physical ranges and flags fits that "succeeded numerically but lied physically."

At deployment (on-line, in the fab)

  • Inline virtual metrology. The surrogate is evaluated per-wafer using FDC (fault detection & classification) sensor traces — chamber pressure, RF power, gas flow integrals, pyrometer profile — to predict $R_s$ and $X_j$ before the wafer ever reaches the four-point-probe or SIMS step. Coverage goes from ~1% sampled to 100% predicted.
  • Run-to-run (R2R) control. A controller agent reads predicted-vs-target offsets and dispatches small recipe tweaks (e.g. $\Delta T$ = +2 K, $\Delta t$ = -0.3 s) wafer-by-wafer to hold $R_s$ on its $C_{pk}$ target. The constrained optimizer in Step 4 is the inner loop.
  • Drift & chamber-matching detection. An anomaly agent monitors the residual NN's output magnitude per chamber. When residuals on one chamber drift away from the fleet median, it flags a maintenance event before the SPC chart does.
  • Conversational fab co-pilot. A retrieval-augmented LLM gives process engineers natural-language access to the surrogate: "What's the cheapest recipe change that brings lot A47 back inside the $R_s$ window without violating thermal budget?" The LLM frames the question; the constrained optimizer answers it; the LLM explains the answer.
  • Yield-impact propagation. A planning agent chains the RTA surrogate into downstream device-level models ($V_t$, $I_{on}$, leakage) so that an out-of-spec $R_s$ is converted into a parametric-yield delta, not just a process excursion.

What we deliberately do not let the agents do

  • Touch the recipe directly without a human-approved change-control ticket on lots that are not engineering-flagged.
  • Replace a calibrated compact model with a black-box surrogate that lacks a physical interpretation, except for residual correction.
  • Extrapolate KPIs into recipe regions where the surrogate's uncertainty exceeds the SPC tolerance.
